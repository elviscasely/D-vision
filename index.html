The studio

Made in
the 770.

A community of creators exploring what moves them, and sharing it through film.

D'Vision Motion Pictures started in Miami with a simple idea: if you have something you care about, point a camera at it. Today the studio calls Kennesaw, Georgia home, and the work comes from the streets, people and light of Kennesaw, Marietta and Atlanta, and from whatever the crew is obsessed with that season.

Everything is independent. The films are written, shot and cut by the people who live with them, then released for anyone to watch. That is what being a visionary means here: see it first, then make it real.
